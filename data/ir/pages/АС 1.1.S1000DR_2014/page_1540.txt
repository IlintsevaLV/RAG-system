<description>
<para>Two changes will be made to the courier job to better

</description>
</lcJobDesign>
<lcPersonalDevelopment>
<description>
<para>Bike couriers that are failing to meet the defined time

limit, they will no longer be required to perform these task

</description>
</lcPersonalDevelopment>
<lcHumanResourceDevelopment>
<description>

</description>
</lcHumanResourceDevelopment>
