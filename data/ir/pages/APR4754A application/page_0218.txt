C 5 ARP4754Α to DO-297 Objectives Comparison
This section summarizes α comparison of the objectives and control categories between ARP4754Α and
DO297. DO-297 does not vary satisfaction of objectives or CM control category by assurance level within
its guidance. All objectives identified are “Recommended” for accomplishment at the assigned
configuration control category due to the Integrated Modular Avionic (IMA) system construction.
There is no Development Assurance Level specified under DO-297; therefore, the comparison will focus
on ARP4754Α assignment level Α for objective coverage and CM category.
Objective Commonality:
1. The Development Process Objectives for identifying requirements and architectures are consistent per
CM category 1 between ARP4754Α and DO297. There are differences identified at Α/C level
objectives (see Objective Differences 2 & 3).
2. The Validation Objectives for V&V data are consistent to be CM category 2 between ARP4754Α and
DO297. There are differences in terms of having objectives (see Objective Differences 4).
3. The Verification Objectives for V&V data are consistent between ARP4754Α and DO297 with minor
considerations (see Objective Differences 5).
4. In general, CM objectives and control categories are consistent between ARP4754Α and DO297. The
CM objectives are always control categories 2 unless related to baseline (configuration) or
environment (platform).
5. In general, Process Assurance objectives are consistent between ARP4754Α and DO297 and
managed under control category 2 with single exception (see Objective Differences 6).
Objective Differences
The ARP4754Α objectives differ from DO297 objectives in α few notable areas.
1. Certification Plan objectives for ARP4754Α and all the Planning Objectives for DO297 are maintained
under CM category 1 while the remaining Planning objectives for ARP4754Α are maintained under CM
category 2.
Analysis 1: As α result of this discrepancy, it is conceivable that DO297 Planning Objectives may need to
align with ARP4754Α objectives to have all the objectives to be maintained under CM category 2 except
for Certification Plan.
2. The ARP4754Α Development Process Objectives of Α/C & system integration is maintained under CM
category 2 while in DO297, most of the objectives of Α/C & system integration data managed per CM
category 1. DO297 also have platform integration and V&V results data controlled at CM category 2.
Analysis 2: As α result of this discrepancy, it is conceivable that ARP4754 Integration Objectives may
need to align with DO297 objectives to have most of the objectives to be maintained under CM category 2
except for platform integration and V&V results.
3. Based on Objective tables under DO297, it is unclear if the Development Process Objectives of
identifying Α/C requirements and allocating Α/C functions to systems are covered or not. The Α/C
function allocation objectives are actually mixed with system function allocation objectives under
DO297 section 3.2, but DO297 Α/C requirements identification equivalent objectives is not identified.
Analysis 3: As α result of this discrepancy, it is conceivable that DO297 objectives of Α/C function
allocation and identification may need to be independent from system objectives in Chapter 3.
4. The ARP4754Α contains two additional Validation Objectives, justifying assumptions and providing
validation substantiation which do not have DO297 equivalent objectives.
