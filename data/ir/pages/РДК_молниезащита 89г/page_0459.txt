Studies of large MOV disks, such as are used in
power transmission systems [17.2, 17.3], have shown
the overshoot to be largely α matter of diffusion of
current into the varistor material. Studies of small
disks, such those as used for protection of low voltage
equipment [17.1] have shown the time dependence of
voltage to be only α few tens of percent, even for surge
currents having fronts of α few nanoseconds.
The fact that surge currents of moderate steep.
ness result in rapidly changing MOV voltages should
be recognized in circuit design. Circuit oscillations re-
suilting from the rapid change in voltage may give rise
to unexpected interference problems.
