Significant conclusions: The most important point
shown by tests on CFC aircraft are that fairly large
voltages can be developed on wiring and that fairly
large currents can flow on metal objects inside the air-
craft. Large currents also can flow through protective
devices when they act to limit overvoltages, particu-
larly if the protective devices are connected between
line and aircraft structure' Stated another way, this
means the surge impedance of the circuits in air- CFC
craft may be very low.
