5.2.3 a Provision of Safe, Controlled Path for Lightning Currents
Since the aircraft is part of the lightning current path between two
external charge centers, lightning currents must flow through the airframe
between attachment points. In most cases aluminum skins and structural
elements provide an excellent conductive path without the need of additional
provisions for lightning currents. Nonmetallic materials, however, such as
