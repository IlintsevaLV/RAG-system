quantity probe, care must be taken to apply test voltage to all the electrode of
combinations which may exist (this need not all be done simultaneously). In
most cases both the "high" and "low" electrodes in such a device are insulated
from "ground" (the airframe), as shown in the simplified drawing of Figure

proximity to the airframe. Thus, the test voltage should be applied across each
of the basic electrode combinations shown in Figure 6.40. Usually, the gaps to
