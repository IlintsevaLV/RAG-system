If the aperture under consideration is not elliptical, a corresponding
equivalent elliptical aperture can generally be specified, as shown in Figure 11.9.
The equivalent elliptical aperture would have the same area and the same
eccentricity as those of the aperture under study. If the aperture were of an
irregular shape, its area might have to be determined numerically.
