On the other hand, a conductive coating has the important advantage of
providing an overall electrostatic shield that will virtually eliminate internal
electric fields and streamering. Thus, if metallic objects must be located inside
the tank in such a manner that streamers are of concern, it may be advisable to
consider a conductive coating over the entire nonmetallic skin. This precaution is
particularly appropriate if occasional burnoff of part of the coating is acceptable
from a maintenance standpoint.
