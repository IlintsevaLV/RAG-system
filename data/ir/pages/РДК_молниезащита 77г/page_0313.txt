10.2 Elliptical Cylinders
If we consider a cylinder of other than circular shape, conditions may be
considerably different. Figure 10.9 shows an elliptical cylinder into which a
step-function current is injected. As previously, we will assume that the cylinder
is long enough that all end effects may be neglected, that it is short compared to
the wavelengths of any frequency components of the injected current, and that
the return path for the current is far enough removed that no proximity effects
need be considered.
